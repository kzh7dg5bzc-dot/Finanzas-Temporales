Privacy Policy

Finanzas Personales is a private, non-commercial application intended exclusively for personal financial management by its owner.

The application accesses financial account information solely for the purpose of allowing its owner to retrieve, organize and analyze their own financial data.

The application is not offered to third parties and does not sell, share or commercially exploit personal or financial data.

Financial information retrieved by the application is intended to be stored locally on the owner's personal computer.

Third-party services required to provide Open Banking connectivity may process data as necessary to provide their respective services, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable law.

For privacy-related enquiries, please contact the email address registered with the application provider.
